# Fuel plans are out

VO2 no longer stores GPX routes, predicts them, or builds fuel plans from MNSTRY products. The Fuel page, the product catalog, and the `route_plans` table go with them.

A second kind of plan — what to eat and drink on an uploaded route — sat beside the training week without feeding it. Routes never counted toward the week target, the calendar, or training load ([0017](0017-fuel-plans-are-stored-per-route.md)). Dropping them leaves the week, calendar, and load as they were.

Weight stays: it is still used for watts per kilogram on personal bests.

This supersedes [0017](0017-fuel-plans-are-stored-per-route.md).

# The calendar shows training weeks, not a civil month

Superseded by [0013](0013-calendar-shows-one-civil-month.md).

The calendar is a diary of completed activities. It shows training weeks (Monday to Sunday), ending with the current one by default: four on desktop, one on phones. There is no toggle between them. It pages one training week at a time and never past the current one. There is no month, day, agenda, or hourly view, and no week-start setting.

A civil month is nearly empty on the 1st, and its rows ignore the Monday-to-Sunday week that planning uses. Activities are drawn as all-day entries, so an hourly grid only adds empty hours. A phone shows one week as a readable day list; four weeks there is a long scroll. This pairs with ADR-0004: remaining work lives in the week bucket, which has its own page, and the calendar only shows what was done.

The cost is no "October at a glance". Older weeks are still one click per week away.

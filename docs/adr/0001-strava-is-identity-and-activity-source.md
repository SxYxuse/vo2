# Strava is identity and the activity source

vo has no email-and-password account. An athlete signs in with Strava, and every activity comes from that athlete's Strava account. There is no second identity, and no other import path.

The alternative was a local login plus optional Strava linking. That would mean a user who is not an athlete, duplicate profiles, and a sync identity separate from the person who trained. Strava-only keeps the product to one person and one source of completed work. The cost is lock-in: without Strava, vo has nothing to show.

# The athlete gives the coach a standing brief, which the coach chat may change through the athlete

Supersedes, in part, [0038](0038-the-coach-chat-can-propose-plan-changes-the-athlete-applies.md): the coach chat now changes the coach plan and the coach brief, each only through a change the athlete applies.

The coach brief is one short text the athlete writes in Settings → Training, such as "Fridays: 30 min at most", "no track nearby", or "knee: no downhill repeats". Every writing of the coach plan reads it, and so does the coach chat. It is optional, at most 500 characters, and not asked in onboarding; athletes from before it have none.

It adds no days. Sessions still have none ([0048](0048-the-coach-plans-sessions-have-no-day-or-order.md)): "Fridays: 30 min at most" means one of the week's sessions fits in 30 minutes, and the athlete places it on Friday. It is advisory: the weekly budget and the coach plan's rules always win, and the coach says in one sentence what it couldn't fit, shown on Plan until the next writing. A changed brief, cleared included, rewrites the weeks ahead like availability, goals, and paces ([0040](0040-the-coach-plan-is-written-when-the-app-asks-and-short-weeks-allow-a-bigger-long-run-share.md)). It is kept when the planning style is off.

In the coach chat, the coach may offer a brief change: the whole brief before and after, with Apply and Decline. Nothing is saved until the athlete taps Apply, and only if the brief is still the one shown. It is for context that holds from now on; a change to one week stays a plan change.

Athletes have context that a number of sessions a week can't say. Time per day ([0041](0041-coach-plan-availability-has-no-time-per-day.md)) and coach notes ([0051](0051-the-coach-plan-takes-changes-only-from-the-coach-chat.md)) were removed: the first asked for a number on every day, the second was a second way to ask for one change. The brief is neither: one optional standing text, read every time, and never a request to change the plan now.

## Considered options

- **Bring back days and time per day:** exact, but undoes [0048](0048-the-coach-plans-sessions-have-no-day-or-order.md) and asks every athlete for what most don't need.
- **A note per week:** covers one-off weeks, which the coach chat's plan changes already do.
- **Check the brief in code:** free text can't be checked, so the rules stay the only hard limits.

# Field tests sit outside the week bucket and are flagged by the athlete

FTP, beep test, and run LTHR are published field tests, not ordinary planned sessions. They are suggested when form is at or above zero, and they are never mixed into the week bucket. After the athlete does one, they open the activity in vo and flag it. vo does not trust the Strava title.

Auto-detecting tests from names, duration, or "best 20-min power" would mis-score hard group rides and races. Flagging is a deliberate extra step so the protocol and the result stay honest. The trade-off is that an unflagged test does not update FTP or LTHR.

# Cycling sessions

The rides VO2 writes, their step format, and how to size them. Every step is a time (`durationS`) and a zone or an effort. A block repeats its steps: 3 × (12 min threshold, 5 min recovery) is one block with repeat 3 and two steps. Warm-up and cool-down are their own blocks. Size every ride from the week's budget and the rules in `principles.md`; when an example is bigger than the budget allows, take repeats off.

## Kinds

| Kind        | Name              | Structure                                                                                                                  | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `recovery`  | Recovery ride     | 30–60 min `recovery`                                                                                                       | After a hard day or an event                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `easy`      | Endurance ride    | 1–3 h `endurance`                                                                                                          | The bulk of the week                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `long`      | Long ride         | 2–6 h `endurance`, some `tempo` late in build for long events                                                              | Fuelling rehearsal                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `sweetSpot` | Sweet spot        | 15 min warm-up; 3–4 × 10–15 min `sweetSpot`, 5 min `recovery`; building to 2 × 20                                          | 60–90 min ([TrainerRoad](https://www.trainerroad.com/sweet-spot-training))                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `tempo`     | Threshold         | 15 min warm-up; 3 × 10–12 min or 2 × 15–20 min `threshold`, recovery about a quarter of the work                           | 20–40 min of work                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| `intervals` | VO2 max intervals | 15 min warm-up; 5 × 4 min or 4 × 8 min `vo2max`, recovery about equal to the work (2 min after 8)                          | 12–32 min of work; 4 × 8 did best in Seiler's 2013 comparison                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `intervals` | Short intervals   | 3 sets of 13 × (30 s `vo2max` to `anaerobic`, 15 s `recovery`), 3 min between sets                                         | Raised VO2 max more than 4 × 5 min ([Rønnestad 2015](https://paulogentil.com/pdf/Short%20intervals%20induce%20superior%20training%20adaptations%20compared%20with%20long%20intervals%20in%20cyclists%20%E2%80%93%20An%20effort-matched%20approach.pdf)) |
| `hills`     | Climbing          | Repeats on a climb at the event's climb length: 3–5 × 8–20 min `threshold` or `sweetSpot`; some at low cadence (50–65 rpm) | Low cadence strains knees; a tool, not race cadence                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `sprints`   | Sprints           | Endurance ride with 6–10 × 10–15 s `sprint`, full recovery (3–5 min)                                                       | Leg speed in base; crits                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

Over-unders (8–12 min alternating 2 min at the top of `threshold` and 1 min `vo2max`) are written as `tempo`. Anaerobic work, 2 × (3 × 1 min `anaerobic`, 1 min easy), as `intervals`.

## By event

Match the work to the event the season builds toward (guidance; [Roadman](https://roadmancycling.com/answers/training-for-a-hill-climb), [FasCat](https://www.fascatcoaching.com/tips/race-specific-cycling-intervals/), [TrainingPeaks](https://trainingpeaks.com/blog/youre-training-too-hard-for-criteriums-heres-why)):

- **Gran fondo or century, flat or rolling:** long rides, tempo and sweet spot; threshold in build.
- **Hilly or mountain gran fondo** (about 15 m of climbing per km or more): climbing repeats as long as the event's climbs, sweet spot and threshold at climb length, back-to-back long weekends when the long ride can't grow further.
- **Gravel:** long endurance base, 3–8 min threshold hill repeats, surges over short pitches; a long event favours steady hours, a short one repeatable hard efforts.
- **Road race or crit:** a solid threshold, then over-unders, 1-minute efforts, and sprints. Too much high intensity erodes endurance.
- **Time trial:** long threshold, building to 2 × 20 min, ideally in the TT position. Intervals about half the race time.
- **Mountain bike (XC):** short climbs and surges, 20–90 s at `anaerobic`, on top of threshold.
- **Hill climb:** threshold or VO2 max efforts at the climb's length.

## Sizing by level

- Under 5 h a week: one quality ride, short (2 × 10 min sweet spot, 5 × 3 min VO2 max).
- 5–8 h: one to two quality rides of 60–90 min.
- 8–12 h: two quality rides; the long ride 3–4 h.
- Over 12 h: two, sometimes three quality rides; the long ride up to 5–6 h.

Fewer repeats in a lighter or taper week, never a new kind of session.

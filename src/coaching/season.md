# A season of runs, rides, and swims

The athlete's plan can hold runs, rides, and swims, toward goals of any of them. VO2 sets a separate budget for each: kilometres of running, minutes of riding, metres of swimming. You write each sport's sessions inside its own budget, by its own knowledge file.

## How VO2 splits the season (rules)

- **The main goal's sport builds.** Each week, the sports of the event the season builds toward (the next main goal) grow with the phase. A supporting race never leads the season. The other sports hold where they are, unless the athlete set a progression for them: their budget doesn't grow, so they keep the fitness the athlete has.
- **Lighter weeks, the taper, and recovery apply to every sport.** In the main goal's taper, cut the other sports first: keep their sessions easy and short.
- **At most 3 quality sessions a week across runs, rides, and swims**, races included (but not races for fun), besides each sport's own limit.

## Writing a mixed week (guidance)

- Make the main goal's sport carry the week's quality work. In the other sports, write mostly easy sessions; one quality session at most in each, and none in peak and taper weeks.
- Cycling transfers to running partly and mostly aerobically: easy rides can add aerobic load for a runner, but never replace the long run or the quality runs. Runners building for a race keep at least three runs a week when their availability allows ([RunnersConnect](https://runnersconnect.net/incorporating-cycling-into-marathon-training/)).
- Running adds impact and recovery cost a ride doesn't: in a cycling build, keep runs easy and short, and never put a hard run in the same week as two hard rides.
- Swimming carries no impact and little leg fatigue, so easy swims fit any week and help recovery ([Bales 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23147088/)). It builds little running or riding fitness; never let it replace a long run or long ride. Its cost is the shoulders (_Swimming_).
- A supporting race in another sport follows its effort, as in that sport's knowledge file: for fun, it is an easy session; strong and steady, that sport's other sessions in its week are easy.
- Two goals of different sports within four to six weeks: the earlier one is best raced as a supporting race unless the athlete made both main goals.
- After a long ride event, a few easy days before the run build resumes.

## Triathlon and duathlon

A multisport event is one goal per leg on the same date, so every leg's sport is the main goal's sport: all of them build. VO2 plans the swims, rides, and runs, each in its own budget.

- **Split the training like the race:** rides about half the time, runs and swims the rest, usually a fifth to a third each. Give one or two more sessions to the athlete's weakest sport, and at least two a week to each ([TrainingPeaks](https://trainingpeaks.com/blog/balancing-swim-bike-run-in-triathlon-training)). Two to three swims a week is typical; for a short race, a weak swimmer gains most from a third.
- **Quality across three sports:** the cap of 3 means at most one quality session per sport in most weeks. A strong swimmer can trade the quality swim for a technique or open water swim and give the slot to the bike or run.
- **Bricks:** once a week in build and peak, end a ride with a short run straight after: write it as the ride, then a separate run of 10–30 minutes, and say in both whys that they go together.
- **Open water:** for an open water race, in build and peak, make one swim a week an open water swim when the athlete has open water, in the race wetsuit; otherwise practise sighting in the pool (_Swimming_).
- **Taper:** swimming is the quickest to recover from, so its taper can start last and keep its frequency; the run carries the most fatigue and is cut first (Friel, by interview, [220 Triathlon](https://www.220triathlon.com/training/tapering-why-it-s-important-and-what-you-should-do-the-week-before-your-triathlon)). Keep each sport's sessions in race week short, with a few race-pace touches.

A duathlon has no swim leg: swims, if the athlete has any, hold as the other sport.

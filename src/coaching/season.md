# A season of runs and rides

The athlete's plan can hold runs and rides, toward goals of either sport. VO2 sets a separate budget for each: kilometres of running, minutes of riding. You write each sport's sessions inside its own budget, by its own knowledge file.

## How VO2 splits the season (rules)

- **The main goal's sport builds.** Each week, the sports of the event the season builds toward (the next main goal) grow with the phase. A supporting race never leads the season. The other sport holds where it is: its budget doesn't grow, so it keeps the fitness the athlete has.
- **Lighter weeks, the taper, and recovery apply to both sports.** In the main goal's taper, cut the other sport first: keep its sessions easy and short.
- **At most 3 quality sessions a week across runs and rides**, races included (but not races for fun), besides each sport's own limit.

## Writing a mixed week (guidance)

- Make the main goal's sport carry the week's quality work. In the other sport, write mostly easy sessions; one quality session at most, and none in peak and taper weeks.
- Cycling transfers to running partly and mostly aerobically: easy rides can add aerobic load for a runner, but never replace the long run or the quality runs. Runners building for a race keep at least three runs a week when their availability allows ([RunnersConnect](https://runnersconnect.net/incorporating-cycling-into-marathon-training/)).
- Running adds impact and recovery cost a ride doesn't: in a cycling build, keep runs easy and short, and never put a hard run in the same week as two hard rides.
- A supporting race in the other sport follows its effort, as in that sport's knowledge file: for fun, it is an easy session; strong and steady, that sport's other sessions in its week are easy.
- Two goals of different sports within four to six weeks: the earlier one is best raced as a supporting race unless the athlete made both main goals.
- After a long ride event, a few easy days before the run build resumes.

## Triathlon and duathlon

A multisport event is one goal per leg on the same date. VO2 plans its runs and rides; swims are the athlete's own. Split the training like the race: rides about half the time, runs about a quarter to a third ([TrainingPeaks](https://trainingpeaks.com/blog/balancing-swim-bike-run-in-triathlon-training)). Once a week in build and peak, end a ride with a short run straight after (a brick): write it as the ride, then a separate run of 10–30 minutes, and say in both whys that they go together.
